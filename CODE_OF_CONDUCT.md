# Code of conduct

Eave welcomes participation regardless of background, identity, ability, or experience.

Treat people with respect. Discuss ideas and code constructively, accept reasonable feedback, and help others participate. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable.

These expectations apply to project discussions, issues, pull requests, and spaces where someone represents Eave. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or remove access when needed. They should act fairly, explain decisions when appropriate, and protect reporters' privacy.

Report concerns privately through a contact method a maintainer has explicitly published. If no private route is available, ask for one without naming affected people or describing sensitive details in a public issue. Contact another maintainer when a concern involves the person who would receive the report. No dedicated conduct-reporting address has been designated yet.
